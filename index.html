Our Story

A Baltimore institution of warmth, craft, and flavor.

David Chu’s China Bistro has welcomed guests on Reisterstown Road for nearly three decades. We cook the dishes families grew up with — Peking duck, handmade dumplings, fragrant soups — with the care of a fine dining kitchen and the comfort of a neighborhood table.

Every plate is prepared to Star-K kosher standards, using fresh ingredients and time-honored technique. Whether you are celebrating, hosting, or simply craving a perfect wonton soup, we treat your visit as an occasion.

  • Star-K Kosher certified kitchen
  • Dine-in, takeout & delivery within 3–4 miles
  • Private dining for gatherings of 8–40 guests
